Nimbus can live in the menu bar only, disconnect when you're not using it, and show and free the space your Finder drives take on this Mac.

## Requirements

- macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon or Intel
- rclone. If none is installed, Nimbus offers to download the official build for your Mac and checks it before use. Share links and backup previews need rclone 1.76 or later.

## Install

From 1.1.0 or later: choose Nimbus > Check for Updates…, or wait for the automatic check if you turned it on.

With Homebrew: `brew trust --tap casavargas/tap && brew tap CasaVargas/tap && brew install --cask nimbus`, or `brew upgrade --cask nimbus` if you installed it that way.

From 1.0.1 or earlier: open the DMG, drag Nimbus to Applications, launch it. The app is signed with a Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple, so Gatekeeper opens it without warnings. Nothing to redo.

## New

- **Show in Dock.** Turn it off in Settings > General and Nimbus lives in the menu bar only. Windows still open from the menu bar; they just don't add a Dock icon.
- **Disconnect when idle.** Settings > General > Connection. After 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes with no Nimbus window open, no transfer or backup running and no network drive mounted, Nimbus stops its connection to your clouds. Opening Nimbus, its menu bar menu, or one of its drives in Finder connects it again; a backup or Shortcut that needs it does the same. Off (Never) by default.
- **Open Nimbus from Finder.** If Nimbus isn't running and you open one of its drives in Finder, it starts in the menu bar so the drive works, without opening a window. On by default; turn it off in Settings > General > Connection.
- **Storage.** A new Settings tab shows how much space downloaded files take on each Finder drive, with Remove Downloads for one drive or all of them. Files stay in your clouds and in Finder, and download again when you open them. Files you keep downloaded aren't removed, and neither are files that are open or not yet uploaded.

## Known issues

- Some Google Docs, Sheets and Slides files show an enormous size in Finder. Nothing is downloaded; the size just isn't known until the file is exported.
- After quitting Nimbus in the middle of a backup, the next run can fail once on a leftover temporary file. Run it again and it completes.
- A backup started by hand and cut off by quitting is not marked as stopped; its row still shows the previous run.
